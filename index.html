// Featured Project

DETECTOR MODULE

Social Distancing Detection in Real Time with AI

A computer-vision-based system designed to analyze live camera feeds or pre-recorded videos to monitor and maintain social distancing standards automatically. The pipeline processes video frames, detects individuals, tracks their movement, and computes spatial distances using perspective transformations.

Key Components & Workflow:

  • YOLOv3 Object Detection: Real-time detection of people in frame.
  • SORT Tracking: Assigns unique IDs to track individual trajectories across frames.
  • Bird’s Eye View Transformation: Applies perspective transformation to map camera coordinates into a top-down planar view for accurate distance calculation.
  • Alerting Mechanism: Highlights proximity violations visually with warning markers when safe distance thresholds are breached.
